# Nushi's design guide

The one page to read before any work on the app's UI: what to draw a view with, and the rules it keeps. It's distilled from the code (`app/lib/theme/`, `app/lib/controls*.dart`, `app/lib/widgets.dart`) and the ADRs, which say why; it adds no rule of its own. When a UI rule changes, change this page in the same commit.

Three things hold it:

- **`tools/design-check`** (in `make lint-dart`) fails on code that goes around the shared pieces: a raw `Tooltip(`, a Material button, an `AlertDialog(` outside `nushiDialog`, a tooltip's shortcut in brackets or after spaces, a `Color(0x…)` or `Colors.<name>` (not `transparent`), a `fontSize:` number, a `Duration(milliseconds: …)` (`Motion` or `Waits` instead; in the controls' files too), a `SnackBar(` or `SnackBarAction(` (`showToast` instead), a second press path (`InkWell(`, `InkResponse(`, `ListTile(`, a `GestureDetector(` that takes a tap (`onTap:`, `onTapUp:`, `onTapDown:` or `onDoubleTap:`; a right-click's or a drag's passes), a `FocusableActionDetector(` or `PressWatch(` outside `controls_press.dart`: `Tap` or `Pressable` instead; in the controls' files too; the rows still pressed by hand are marked `// design: to move onto Tap`). A deliberate use takes `// design: <why>` on its line or alone on the line above.
- **`tools/design-check`** fails on a corner that isn't a radius token (ADR [0110](adr/0110-corners-come-from-the-theme.md)) and on a size off the type scale; **`tokens_test.dart`** holds every theme's text to AA on its fills, and a field's hint to 3:1 and a clear step under its value.
- **The gallery**: `make gallery` draws every shared control in every state, one sheet per group, dark and light, to `app/build/gallery/` (`app/test/gallery/sheets.dart`); `gallery_test.dart` keeps the buttons', the small controls' and the fields' sheets as goldens, so a change to a component shows in review. A sheet forces each state with `ShowLook`. Add a new shared piece to a sheet.

## Tokens

### Colours (`theme/tokens.dart`, ADR [0086](adr/0086-color-themes.md), [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md))

Every colour is a token of `Tokens`; a theme changes values, never names. Read them as `NushiTheme.of(context).t`.

**Surfaces**, told apart by fill, a step at a time, never by an outline (ADR [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md)):

| Token | Fills |
|---|---|
| `window` | the window behind everything; the sidebar sits on it |
| `base` | a pane: the main area, a phone's screen |
| `raised` | a card (`cardBox`), a segmented track, what floats (`floatBox`: a menu, a dialog, a popover) |
| `lifted` | a raised bit on a card: a button, a menu field, a key's cap, a tooltip, a toast, a meter's track |
| `segment` | the picked segment of `Segmented` |
| `codeBlock` | code, sunk: a code block, a tool call's output, a diff |
| `selected` | a list's selected row (the keyboard's place) |
| `hover`, `pressed` | the tint over anything under the pointer, and while it's pressed |
| `quote` | quoted text's fill, in place of a bar down its side |

**Lines**: `line` is a hairline, only a divider inside a pane (under a header, between rows) and around the composer; `floatLine` the edge of what floats. A border stays only on a text field (`fieldBorder`, `fieldBorderHover`; `focus` with the caret, `crashed` in error), as the focus ring, and as the composer's ring in `focus` (the accent's ink, which keeps 3:1 on code's fill in every set) in command mode, on `codeBlock`'s fill, drawn inside its edge so nothing moves (`command_mode.dart`, ADR [0148](adr/0148-a-message-starting-with-bang-runs-a-command.md)).

**Text**, from strongest to faintest, three greys a clear step apart (at least 6 in L*, `tokens_test`; `textMuted` sits at AA on the fills it's on, `textSecondary` halfway to `text`). A named theme keeps its published colours, so four sets keep two greys nearly as one: Tokyo Night's (its own fg_dark and #9AA5CE; Day's text and AA floor sit together), Catppuccin Latte's (its Text, and Subtext1 and Subtext0 taken to AA land on it) and Solarized's dark set (its greys taken to AA land within 7 of each other).

| Token | For |
|---|---|
| `text` | body text, a value, a title, an open row's label |
| `textSecondary` | a row's title at rest, a label beside a value, a tooltip's words, an icon button's icon |
| `textMuted` | meta and small print: an age, a count, a description under a row, an empty state |
| `hint` | a field's hint, an example of what goes in it: `textMuted` taken toward the field's fill to 3.5:1 (`hintOn(fill)` for a box on another fill, as the composer's), a clear step under a typed value, so it never reads as typed |
| `faint` | a grey mark that stands back: the empty window's logo, a rail's dashes, the part of a meter that counts for nothing |
| `link` | `LinkText` only |
| `onAccent` | on the accent's fill |

**The accent**: `accent` fills (a primary button, a switch that's on; `accentPressed` pressed), `accentSoft` is its tint (the open row, a lit toggle, a choice to make), `accentInk` is the accent as text, an icon, or the focus ring (`focus`), since `accent` is too dark for that on dark surfaces.

**States**, each an ink and a soft fill for its pill (`PhasePill`): `running` (on `accentSoft`), `needs`/`needsSoft` (waiting on the user; a warning), `done`/`doneSoft`, `crashed`/`crashedSoft` (failed; destructive), `idle` (off). Asleep has no mark in a list: the dot's slot stays empty and the row's title and agent mark take `textMuted` at half strength and the title is italic (`recededColor`, `recededTitle`: fainter than an archived chat's, since muted alone read like an idle row; a moon beside the word Asleep in the chat's header only). A state that asks for nothing is `textSecondary` on `raised`. A chat's dot (`ChatDot`, `PhaseDot` for a collapsed group; the sidebar, the phone's list) tells each state by shape as well as colour, so it reads with Reduce Motion on and without its tooltip: a ring in `idle` (idle), a `running` dot with a ring growing out of it (working), two `running` dots side by side, which every 3.9 seconds spin clockwise twice round as drops of liquid, slowly and then faster, squashed and stretched by their way, each a teardrop trailing its tail, spiral in and merge into one drop, then spiral out as two again through a neck that parts and wobble into their places like jelly (in background: `BackgroundMark`; still at rest, with Reduce Motion, in the back and in a collapsed group, where it's the two dots; its outlines traced once and kept, so a frame of its play draws one small path and its rest asks for no frames), a solid `done` dot (an end unseen), the `needs` dot a size up in its soft ring (needs you).

**Selected text**: `textSelection`, the accent's tint behind selected text everywhere (the transcript, a field, the editor, a file's source, the terminal); a diff's code takes it a step stronger (`diffTheme`), so it shows over a changed line's tint, or the theme's own published selection (`DiffColors.textSelection`, Tokyo Night's bg_visual).

**Shadows**: `shadow`, and `floatShadow` under what floats (twice as deep on a dark set); `scrim` behind an image opened whole.

**The transcript's own**: `userBand*` (your message's bubble), `agentText`, `inlineCode`/`inlineCodeText`, `toolAccent`, `subagentTint`/`subagentAccent`, `findingsAccent`, `selectionBar*`, `shine`/`shineBand` (what's still going). **Marks**: `claude`, `ante`, `ompPlug`, `pi`, `pig`, `monogramText`, `gold`, `avatars` (people's and chats' colours). **Code**: `syntax` by `Capture`, `terminal` (ANSI), `diff`. Code on a diff's changed line or changed word keeps the contrast it has on the plain background, up to 3:1, by its lightness alone, so it keeps its hue (`codeOnTintContrast`, ADR [0086](adr/0086-color-themes.md)'s amendment); everywhere else a theme's code colours are as published.

A new colour is a new token, with a value in every built-in theme and a line in `theme_file.dart` (ADR [0086](adr/0086-color-themes.md)).

### Type (`NushiTheme.ui`, `NushiTheme.code`, ADR [0114](adr/0114-app-and-code-fonts.md))

Text is set with `theme.ui(color, size:, weight:)` (the App font, IBM Plex Sans unless picked) or `theme.code(color, size:)` (the Code font, JetBrains Mono unless picked; ligatures off). Never a `TextStyle(fontSize: …)` of a view's own.

| Size | For |
|---|---|
| 20 w600 | a Settings page's title |
| 15 w600 | a dialog's title (the theme's) |
| 14 | the transcript's prose (on 22), the chat header's title (semibold), a large control's label, the composer's text |
| 13 | body text and a control's label (`ControlSize.text`) |
| 12 | meta: a row's description, a muted line, a tooltip, a compact picker |
| 11.5 | small print: a group's name (semibold, `textMuted`), a count in a pill |
| 11 | a key hint (`keyHint`, code) |

Nothing is set between the steps: a size that was (12.5, 13.5, 10.5) went to the nearest, and a tie to the smaller, but a control's label, which is 13 (`PillButton`, a pill `MenuField`, `SendButton`, the selection bar's actions). The theme picker's small picture of the app sets these steps at its own scale. Code is 13 in an editor and 12 in the transcript's blocks and tool rows. The phone's text is larger (`phoneText`, 16; 14 for a bar's words). Labels of controls are medium (w500); an open row's or a picked segment's label is semibold.

### Spacing (`NushiTheme`, ADR [0072](adr/0072-the-chat-areas-spacing.md), [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md))

| Constant | Value | Between |
|---|---|---|
| `windowPad` | 8 | the window's edge and the pane |
| `paneGap` | 8 | panes side by side |
| `gutter` | 24 | a side of the transcript, the composer, the chat header |
| `barGap` | 12 | controls in a bar |
| `groupGap` | 24 | groups of controls in a bar |
| `iconGap` | 2 | icon buttons, which carry their own padding |
| `menuPad` | 5 | a menu's edge and its rows |

Fixed heights: a header `PaneHeader.height` 56 (with a hairline under it, no fill), Changes' bar 44, a tool call's row 36, a sidebar row 34, a settings row 46 at least (a key row 36), a menu row 30, the composer's row 32. The transcript's column is `chatWidth` 800 at most; the details panel 320, between the transcript and the side panes, which run the pane's full height beside the transcript or over it (over it, the transcript, its header and terminal keep their least under them); it tucks away first, then the side panes narrow toward their least, the widest first and never saved, and only at their least do they lie over the transcript (`besideFit`, ADR [0123](adr/0123-side-panes-right-of-the-rail.md)). The phone keeps its own values (`TouchTargets`, every target 44).

### Corners (`NushiTheme`, ADR [0110](adr/0110-corners-come-from-the-theme.md))

Every rounded corner is one of these, or `NushiTheme.inner(outer, gap)` for a shape set inside a rounded one (its parent's radius less the gap, so the curves stay parallel):

| Token | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `tag` | 4 | a key hint, a branch's name, a loading bar, a checkbox |
| `control` | 8 | a compact control, an icon button, a row's hover and selected fill, a tool row, a code block, a diff, an image |
| `card` | 12 | a card, a settings group, a toast |
| `panel` | 14 | a pane, a menu, a dialog, a popover, a message's bubble, the composer |
| `pill` | 999 | round ends: chips, badges, counts, `PillButton`, `RoundButton`, a thin bar |

A row one line high is never a card (12 on 28 makes a lozenge). Nor is a field one line high a regular field: a name edited in place (`InlineEdit`: a chat's title in the sidebar or its header, a terminal tab, a section, the new chat dialog's branch) is a low box with `tag` corners and an edge in `focus`, drawn a few points past the text, so the row keeps its height and the name doesn't move as editing starts; a regular field's corners on it make a pill. What isn't a corner (a dot, a scrollbar's thumb, an icon's geometry) says `// not a corner: <why>`.

### Control sizes (`theme/sizes.dart`, ADR [0122](adr/0122-control-sizes-by-place.md))

A control's size comes from where it sits, never from the call: a `ControlScale` above it says, and without one it's `regular`.

| Size | Height | Label | Ends | Icon | Narrowest | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `compact` | 26 | 13 | 10 | 14 | — | the details panel, a list's or table's row, a menu's row, a one-line toast, a narrow bar |
| `regular` | 30 | 13 | 12 | 14 | — | everywhere else: a bar, a card, a settings row, a form; every text field |
| `large` | 34 | 14 | 16 | 15 | 80 | a dialog's actions (`nushiDialog` sets it), a view's main call to action |
| `touch` | 48 | 16 | 16 | 18 | its row's width | the phone's full-width button (`PhoneButton`) |

`Button`, `MenuButton`, `SplitButton`, `SendButton`, a filled `MenuField`, `Segmented` and `LineSlider` follow it; corners scale with the height. These keep their own sizes, set by what they sit in: the composer's pills and round buttons (32), a tool row's small pills (24), `IconTap` (26; 32 in a header), `WideButton` (38, its corners its height's share all the same: `ControlSize.radiusFor`), menu rows (30), settings rows (46). A new dense place says `ControlScale.compact`, or its buttons grow.

### Motion (`motion.dart`, ADR [0102](adr/0102-loops-run-on-a-pulse-only-in-front.md))

| `Motion.` | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `quick` | 120 ms | a crossfade of text or colour, a button swapping |
| `standard` | 200 ms | size: opening, folding, bars sliding |
| `enter` | 240 ms | coming in: panes, a toast |
| `exit` | 160 ms | going (two-thirds of coming in) |
| `settle` | curve | entering and moving |
| `size` | curve | heights and widths |
| `leave` | curve | leaving |

What waits, where several places wait alike, is `Waits.` (`motion.dart`; not motion, so Reduce Motion leaves it): `loading` 150 ms before a load's loading look shows, `typing` 250 ms of a pause in typing before it's acted on, `slow` 300 ms before a call that shows nothing says it's running, `handover` 600 ms at most that what shows stays while what replaces it loads unseen, `refreshShown` 600 ms at least that a refresh the user asked for (a Refresh, ⌘R) says it's working, so one that changed nothing still shows it ran, `save` 500 ms before a draft is written.

No view writes `Duration(milliseconds: …)`: `tools/design-check` flags it outside `motion.dart` and the theme's files, the controls' included. A duration that is no step of either, its own thing (a loop's beat on `pulseClock`, how long a state shows, a one-off flourish such as a check drawing itself, a hover's grace, a poll), keeps its number with `// design: <why>`.

Input never waits: the state changes at once and motion is only drawn; a second press reverses from where the motion is. Exits are faster than entries. Under `reduceMotion` sizes and places jump (a press's tint stays). Nothing runs at rest: what loops for as long as a state lasts repaints from `pulseClock` (20 Hz, only while Nushi is in front). The transcript's width never eases: lay out once at the final width and glide by a transform.

## Components

Everything clickable is one of these (`controls.dart` and its parts, ADR [0049](adr/0049-controls-look-like-controls.md)); all show the pointing hand, take the keyboard's focus with a ring, press on Enter and Space, show a press at once (before what it does is done; a finger's in a list that scrolls only once held `Waits.pressInScroll`, 70 ms, or as a quicker tap lifts, so a touch that turns into a scroll tints nothing; a mouse's always at once), are a button to a screen reader (`Pressable`'s `role`: a `LinkText` is a link, a `Toggle` a switch; no hand-made `Semantics(button:)`), and are disabled with no `onTap`/`onPressed`. **Plain text is never clickable.**

## Patterns

- **Dialogs**: `nushiDialog`: a title that says what's asked ("Delete this chat?"), content in plain sentences, actions at the right, Cancel first and the one main action last. The main action is `primary`; one that deletes or loses something is `destructive` and says what it does ("Delete", not "OK"). The main action has the focus, unless it deletes or loses something: then Cancel has it, so Enter never deletes, as the Mac's alerts (`confirm(destructive: true)`, the chat's Delete, Clean up). A line saying what ticking a choice would lose ("… would be lost") is in `crashed` ink, never muted, in Delete and Clean up alike. A list to manage has a × and a Done. Esc cancels. While a dialog is open no action from a key or the menu bar runs (Quit and the system's items aside), so nothing changes under what it asks: one place decides, `actsNow` (`app_actions.dart`), by the route on top of the Navigator; the dialog's own keys (Esc, Enter, its shortcuts, its fields' editing) are its own. A picker (⌘K's palette, Artifacts… as a palette, ⌘P's file opener; `picker.dart`, told by its route's settings) asks nothing, but shows one at a time: its own key or menu item closes it, another picker's takes its place, and other actions wait as over a dialog. Each is one box, `PickBox` (`palette.dart`; the rewind list is one too, as a dialog): a field, the rows that match under it in a lazy list, ↑↓ moving the lit row and keeping it in view, ⏎ or a click picking, Esc closing, a muted line in the rows' place when there are none ("Nothing matches", "Listing…", "Stop the turn first"). A filter in its own frame (a long menu's, the Jira issue picker) takes the same keys from `pickKey` (`picker.dart`), and the palette, a menu's filter and the rewind list match each word typed (`matchesWords`).
- **Cards in the transcript** (ADR [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md)): a tool call's row is one line on `raised`, corners `control`; open, a card with its output in a `codeBlock` block. A card that waits on you (a permission, a question, the trust card) is a `cardBox` with an amber mark and `NeedsYou`; its one main answer solid, the rest secondary. A subagent's row (`SubagentLine`) and a workflow's card (`WorkflowCard`, ADR [0127](adr/0127-claudes-workflows-show-in-the-chat.md); one line as a subagent's row, saying each thing once: its name, then where it is and how long; narrow, the state takes a line under the name, and an end that wasn't done has a line of its own; a chevron at its start, as a tool call's, opens it in place to its description and its phases with who runs in each, `WorkflowPhases`, and the corners become a card's; ADR [0139](adr/0139-the-workflow-card-says-each-thing-once-and-opens-in-place.md)) sit on `subagentTint`, `accentSoft` while their pane is open, `crashedSoft` once failed; their state ends the first line, in one order (what it does or how it ended, its count, its tokens, its time; done says no word, its check does), then `TaskMark` (Stop under the pointer while it runs, in the mark's room, so nothing moves) and the pane icon (`paneIcon`: a row that opens a side pane ends in it, a button on the workflow's card, whose click opens it in place; a chevron is only for what opens in place). A row of the Workflow pane's tree has no state words, so a running agent's mark is the running dot (`StatusMark` in `running`), not `TaskMark`'s blank. A phase not reached yet (ADR [0141](adr/0141-a-workflow-shows-its-plan.md)), in the opened card and the pane alike, follows the reached ones with three cues, none of them colour alone: `TaskMark`'s `planned` ring (`StepRing`, a plan's step to come), its title in `textSecondary` at the regular weight, and the word "planned" ("not reached" once the run ended) where a reached one has its count; in the pane it has nothing to fold, so no chevron. Nothing is outlined, and nothing in a card is a card.
- **Empty states**: a muted line where the content would be, in `textMuted`: "None" in a settings group (`SettingsGroup.empty`), "No chats" in a group, "Nothing matches" under a filter, "Not shown: a.png is binary" in place of a file. It's a phrase, never a sentence: no full stop, even when a clause says what to do after a colon ("No inline comments: select lines in Files to add some"). An error or an explanation is a sentence and keeps its stop. A view with nothing open offers its main action large (the empty window's New chat).
- **A running time** ("12s", "1m 04s": a tool row's, a run's, a subagent's) is secondary: 12, `textMuted`, tabular figures so it doesn't jitter as it ticks, at its row's end, one line, never wrapping; in a narrow row the title gives way before it. It ticks on the app's one `secondClock` (`clock.dart`) through `LiveText`, only while live and in its view, never on a timer of its own; an age ("2m ago") ticks on `minuteClock` beside it, the same way.
- **A wide side pane's rows** (the Files pane, the browser; ADR [0123](adr/0123-side-panes-right-of-the-rail.md)): a top row level with the chat's header, its name or tabs at its left and `PaneEndButtons` (Expand, ×) at its right end, 8 in, at the same pixels in each pane; under it the pane's own bar (Files' path line and buttons, Collapse all folders, the tree's button and ⋯ in 32 boxes at its end; the browser's address). A pane's options live in its ⋯ (Files' as Claude's: a row with a key has its check before the key), one pref each, the View menu and the palette showing the same (`view_options.dart`). A pane with nothing open says so in its body as Claude's does: an icon, a line in `text`, a muted line of how to open something (Files' "Open files appear here"). Squeezed in a narrow window, a pane drops what it can (its title, a +, a menu) before its buttons are cut.
- **What loads doesn't move what's around it**: its room is kept from the first frame where it's likely to come (the turn line's between turns; the bar under a new chat's box before its snapshot says it has no messages), and what comes late eases its size (`EaseSize`) and comes in (`Enter`), never pops. What's replaced stays until its replacement is known, rather than blanking or saying "…" a moment: dimmed to half past `Waits.loading` as on its way out, then it fades out in the first part of `standard` and the new fades in in the rest, never the two over each other (the details panel as a chat not seen before is picked; one seen before shows at once, as its folder last stood, and reads it again).
- **Settings rows**: label left (13, `text`), an optional description under it (12, `textMuted`, one line; text that isn't Nushi's own, as a reply card's words, cut to it with `descriptionLines`), the control at the group's right edge; a group is a card with hairlines between rows, its actions (Add a token) at the end of its title's line. A group of one row is folded into its neighbour; two choices a form keeps are `Segmented`, not a menu; an action is a `Button`, never a link. A connection's account is an `AccountRow` (`account_rows.dart`): its icon in a tile, who it is, its state in a `NotePill` (Signed in, Connected), and a ⋯ of the details that are jargon (a token's kind and scopes) and Remove; GitHub's and Jira's alike. Text a user edits that is longer than a line (a reply card's words) is a row with Edit, which opens it in a dialog, and Edited and Reset once changed. A key row (Settings › Keyboard shortcuts, `shortcut_row.dart`) is its own, one line 36 high as desktop apps' keymaps: the name left, the key at the edge as a cap that is the control (`ShortcutField`, `shortcut_field.dart`, a `KeyCapButton`: 24 high on `lifted` (taller with larger text, as the search field), hugging its key, at least 46 wide, code type 12, never scaled; the ring when focused from the keyboard (the keyboard moved last, as a browser's `:focus-visible`), not after a click; "Press keys…" on the accent's soft fill while it listens, growing to the left; a chosen key on the accent's soft fill in `accentInk`; no key "None" on a `fieldBorder` outline with no fill); a click anywhere on the row makes it listen (the row tints under the pointer as a `Tap` does, its cap with it so the cap keeps its edge, but isn't one: the cap is the row's one focus stop). Reset and No key sit left of the cap in slots that keep their room, Reset shown on a changed row, No key only under the pointer or with the focus in the row (still a Tab stop), so the cap never moves; a group's caps take its widest cap's room, each at its right end, so the slots and second keys line up down the group however long each key is (Linux's words). An action's second key is a note, not a cap: "Also" and its keys in small muted code (`keyHint`), left of them at the end of the name's line, or on a line of its own under the name where both don't fit, never left out while it works; a fixed key is the same cap with no hover, hand or focus. A clash (`needs`, with Take it and Cancel at its end, compact), a refusal (`crashed`), a key not used (`needs`) or a note (`textMuted`) is a line under the name, two lines at most, the buttons under it where both don't fit; the row grows for it, its cap stays. Over the list, a search field the column's width (a search icon, a hint, × once it holds text, Esc empties it) stays in view with the title and Reset all while the list scrolls (`SettingsPage.head`), a hairline under them once the list is under them; a search finding nothing says "Nothing matches". Every other state Settings shows is a `NotePill`, never a bare word: an MCP server's (Connected, Failed to connect, Off), an agent's Ready. Copy is short (the owner's rule).
- **The pull request page** (ADR [0137](adr/0137-the-pull-request-tab-follows-githubs-page.md)): GitHub's own layout in Nushi's skin. One scroll of the head (the title at 20 w600, Merge status and Submit review at its row's right end), `PlainTabs` with counts, then the tab; once the title has scrolled away a slim bar (44: state pill, `#n`, title, the same buttons) lies over the scroll's top, its content in the page's column and its buttons at the same right edge, still while it shows (only the whole bar fades in and out, with a few points of slack), so nothing under it moves; 1160 wide at most, centred (`pullPad`), the side column 270 beside the main one, under it below 1000. The merge button is the page's one primary; the merge status card's segments open one list at a time; a person is a mark of their login's first letter (`PersonMark`), never an image from GitHub, and a label's own colour shows only as a dot on Nushi's pill.
- **Headers and bars**: 56 high, a hairline under, no fill; the title semibold 14 at the gutter, icon buttons 32 square at the end, 12 from the edge, a lit or hovered one's fill 2 inside its box (any icon button 28 or more, in every bar) so two side by side keep a gap; the pane buttons (terminal, Files, browser, details) fold into ⋯ together, never one alone; controls `barGap` apart, groups `groupGap`. Empty space in a header moves the window (ADR [0080](adr/0080-no-title-bar.md)). Nothing a button opens changes the width of the bar it's in, but a side pane: it opens at the chat header's right, which it ends, and the header's buttons glide left as it slides in (ADR [0123](adr/0123-side-panes-right-of-the-rail.md)). A side pane's own top row is a header, level with the chat's; narrowed below its least it shortens its words (`PaneTitle`: the caption cut, then gone, then the name), never cutting a button.
- **Drags**: what follows the pointer (a pane's edge) moves only while its primary press is held: never from a trackpad's two-finger scroll or another button, and it ends where its press was let go, seen or not (`Resizable`, ADR [0080](adr/0080-no-title-bar.md)).
- **Hover and focus**: under the pointer a control takes `hover` over its fill (a filled primary goes a shade lighter), pressed `pressed`; focused from the keyboard it wears the 2-point ring in `focus` (`focusRing`); on the accent's fill (a primary button, `SplitButton`, `SendButton`, a primary `RoundButton`) the ring sits just outside it, with a 2-point line of `onAccent` at its edge (`accentFocusRing`), since a light set's `focus` is within a shade of `accent`. A row (`Tap`) shows focus as the hover tint. A toggle that's on is `lit` or `held`, never a different shape.
- **Disabled**: a control without its action is drawn at 45% (`_faded`; `PillLink` too), with no hand and no focus; an `IconTap` greys its icon instead (Claude desktop's Back with nowhere to go); a field's border goes to `line`.
- **Words**: the glossary's (`docs/glossary.md`); sentence case; a shortcut in a tooltip via `withKey`.

## The owner's rules

- **Never a coloured stripe down the side** of a card, row or group: use a chip, an icon or a fill (ADR [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md); a chat's colour is a `ColorChip`, a quote is a `quote` fill). A change mark in a code gutter, beside the lines it marks (the Files pane's source, `ChangeMark`), is an editor's gutter mark, not a card's or a row's stripe: solid, 3 points, in the diff's inks (`done` added, `accentInk` changed, `crashed`'s wedge removed), between the line's number and its text (ADR [0131](adr/0131-change-marks-in-a-files-source.md)). The Blame gutter left of the numbers (`blame_gutter.dart`) tells its runs apart by a hairline (`line`) over each but the first, its words in `textMuted`, never by a fill or a stripe.
- **State cues are unmistakable, not faint tints.** A running server's Stop is solid red (`IconTap(danger:)`); a toggle that's on is `lit`; a waiting card has its mark and a pill; a failed refresh's line ("Couldn't refresh · as of 3m ago", `updatedText`) is in `crashed` after its mark, never muted like a plain time, and a narrow bar says only "Couldn't refresh", the time on hover. A page read from GitHub (a pull request's) always says how fresh it is ("Updated 2m ago"), a spinner before "· refreshing" while it's asked (`updatedText`'s `busy`), so a refresh always shows. The composer's own Stop is the neutral square in a circle the owner kept (ADR [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md)).
- **A show/hide toggle never moves when pressed** (ADR [0111](adr/0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md), [0118](adr/0118-title-buttons-stay-put.md)): the header's buttons and the sidebar's button stay put whatever they show or hide. The one exception: a side pane, opening at the header's right, takes the buttons' place and they move left (ADR [0123](adr/0123-side-panes-right-of-the-rail.md)); the details and the terminal never move them. A card over the composer that opens (the pinned plan, ADR [0135](adr/0135-the-plan-pinned-over-the-composer.md)) keeps its control in the line at its foot and opens above it, since what stands on the composer grows upward.
- **A named third-party theme uses its published colours** and known variants (ADR [0086](adr/0086-color-themes.md)); only Nushi's own roles pick other steps of its palette.
- **Settings copy is short**: a label and at most a line under it.
- **Tooltips are muted, the key fainter, no brackets, one key**: `textSecondary` words on `lifted`, the key in `tooltipKey` after an en space (`withKey`), as Claude desktop draws them. A tooltip names its control and that control's own key; other keys that do something near it (⌃Tab past the header's tabs) are the menu bar's and the palette's to show.
- **Copying Claude desktop means its actual behaviour**, from the app or the owner's screenshots, not a guess at it (ADR [0094](adr/0094-browser-tabs-for-local-web-servers.md)'s amendments).
- **Nushi is unopinionated**: options and warnings over enforced flows.

## Known inconsistencies

None open. The follow-ups found while writing this page and drawing the gallery are fixed: a disabled `IconTap(danger:)` or `IconTap(lit:)` kept its fill and lost its mark, a toast's action was all but invisible on a light set, and the list this section held (a destructive dialog's focus, hints, the greys, the focus ring on the accent, the type scale, durations, empty states' punctuation, the phone's controls, `WideButton`'s corners and `PillLink`'s disabled look, the two kinds of toast). A new one goes here until it's fixed.
