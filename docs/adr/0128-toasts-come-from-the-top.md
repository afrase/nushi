# 0128. Toasts come from the top

Status: accepted, 2026-11-16; amended 2026-11-17 (the update card back at the bottom right, by the owner's ask; toasts stay at the top); amended 2026-11-17 again (the bars take a row of their own, below); amended 2026-11-17 a third time (the card stands above every bar at a chat tab's foot, not only the composer); amended 2026-11-19 (the card always in the window's bottom right corner, below); amended 2026-11-20 (beside the composer where the chat's column leaves it room, below); amended 2026-11-23 (a failure never waits behind a plain toast, below). Amends [0061](0061-the-daemon-checks-for-a-newer-release.md) (where the newer release's card sits) and [0111](0111-surfaces-and-floating-panes.md) (what floats under the pane's header).

## Context
The owner: "Ah maybe it's the toast saying it's checking for updates? It just visually looks like the no updates toast. We should also move the toast to the top instead of bottom." Toasts were Material snack bars at the window's bottom, centred on the whole window (not the main area), over the composer; the update card sat in the bottom right corner, over the same strip, and at 720 wide the two overlapped. "Checking for updates…" and "Nushi is up to date" were the same plain line.

## Decision
- **Toasts show at the top** on the Mac and Linux: 420 wide, centred over the chat's transcript (the composer's centre: what the details rail and a side pane leave of it; in a view that isn't a chat, the main area, right of the sidebar shown or hidden), 8 points under the header, under the bars when one shows. Where the transcript left beside a pane is narrower than a toast and its margins, it's centred over the main area instead; a toast never leaves the main area (8 points in at least, narrowed below 420 when the area is narrower than 436), nor does the card. The bars take a row of their own under the header (amended 2026-11-17, below). Toasts slide down and fade in, and only fade as they go; under Reduce Motion they're there at once.
- **The app's own host**, not a snack bar (Flutter's only sit at the bottom): `ToastHost` above the navigator, with its queue (`Toasts`: one at a time, `replace` takes the showing one's place, a duration, an action holds it under VoiceOver) and an overlay of its own, so a toast is over every view, Settings and a dialog; with no snack bar to clip it, it has the float's shadow, as the update card has. The layout under the header marks the place (`ToastAnchor`, placed by `NoticesPlace` in a chat's `Beside`, and by `BarsRow` under another view's header) and the toast follows it; with no main area (the daemon starting) it's under where the header would be.
- **The update card sits at the transcript's bottom right** (amended 2026-11-17; the owner: "should still be in the bottom right. The other toasts can stay at the top."): 16 in from its right edge and 16 above its composer and turn status, rising as the composer grows, so never over its buttons; on the Changes and Pull request tabs at their bottom right too, and 16 above each bar at the tab's foot it would otherwise cover (the cards waiting in the chat's box, Changes' commit box where a narrow window brings them under it; `CardFloor`): it may lie over a few lines of what scrolls (the transcript, a diff), never over a control; left of the details rail when the rail shows, above the terminal when it's open; at the main area's bottom right in a view that isn't a chat or over an expanded pane. Where the panes lie over the transcript and leave it no room left of them (a narrow window), it's the transcript's and lies under them, hidden until there's room or the pane closes: never a sliver of it, never over a pane's rows. Toasts and bars at the top never meet it, so neither moves for the other.
- **The card is always in the window's bottom right corner** (amended 2026-11-19; the owner, with a screenshot of it floating mid-window left of the details column: "Can we also make this update notice always show in the far right? A little weird it floats there", then "Yes always the bottom right corner"). It replaces the column rule above: 16 in from the main area's right and bottom edges (24 from the window's) whatever is open (the details column, a side pane, a pane expanded over all, Jira, GitHub, an empty window, the sidebar shown or hidden), over a pane or the column when they hold the corner, and never hidden under them. Its left never moves; it only rises, as before, 16 above each bar with controls at the foot of what's under it (`UnderCard`): the chat's composer and turn status, the cards waiting, Changes' commit box, a side chat's composer, the box that messages a subagent. Plain content at a foot (a terminal's text, a file, the details widgets, a page, server logs) it lies over, since it can be dismissed. A view's bar hidden under the panes lying over it (a narrow window), or under a pane expanded over all, doesn't lift it. Where the transcript reaches the corner it's as before, above the composer. The lift is measured where the bars rest as laid out, not as they slide, so it moves once as a pane opens rather than following the slide.
- **A slow call's "running" toast has a spinner, its answer an icon**: Check for updates says "Checking for updates…" with a spinner, then "Nushi is up to date" with a green check (an info icon for no release or a development build), in the same place.
- **The phone keeps its toasts at the bottom**, near the thumb, the same host at the bottom edge.

- **Beside the composer where there's room** (amended 2026-11-20; the owner, with a screenshot of the card floating a composer's height up in a wide window, nothing under it: it should be at the bottom, "not floating over the prompt input"). A chat's foot is as wide as its view, but its composer and the lines over it are only as wide as the chat's column: the card rose above the whole foot, so in a window wider than the column it hung over empty pane. A bar now says how much of each end is bare (`UnderCard.clear`, the column's margin), and the card rises only when it's across from the rest: in a wide window it's in the corner, beside the composer; where the column reaches under it, above it as before.
- **A failure never waits behind a plain toast** (amended 2026-11-23): a slow call's spinner stays up to 5 minutes, a toast with an action holds under VoiceOver, so an error queued behind one went unseen that long. A failure (`showFailure`) takes the place of a plain toast showing at once, `replace` or not (that one is gone), and waits only for a failure showing, ahead of the plain ones waiting. A plain toast never takes a failure's place, `replace` or not, and `hide` (a call taking its own spinner away) leaves a failure: it goes after its 8 s or by its ×.
- **The bars take a row of their own** (amended 2026-11-17): the daemon's lost, back and restarting, its update waiting for idle chats, removed chats and a start walked away from are `TopBar`s, cards on the pane (`raised`, corners `control`, no shadow), in a row under the header that pushes what's under it down as one slides in and gives the room back as it goes. In a chat with its panes beside the transcript, the row is under the chat's header and as wide (the transcript and the details rail go down; the panes' own headers stay level with the chat's and their rows where they were, so the bar reads as the chat's, not one pane's). Where the panes lie over the transcript (a narrow window), the row is across the whole chat under its header, which is then all there, and the panes, a drawer over the transcript, go down under it. Over an expanded pane, the row is across the top and the pane goes down by it (past the title buttons while the sidebar is hidden). Another view (Jira, GitHub, an empty window, the trust card) has the row under its header (`BarsRow`). The owner's screenshot: the update's bar, floating centred under the header, lay over a transcript's first lines and a background command's pane for as long as a chat ran; a toast goes in seconds, a bar can stay for minutes, so a jump of one row once beats covered text. Before 2026-11-12 the bars were a row over the header, across the pane, which ran under the traffic lights and moved the header (0111); under the header, neither happens.

## Consequences
- A toast covers the top of the transcript (or a view's first rows) for its seconds; that part of a chat is what's been read already.
- A bar coming or going moves the transcript (or a view's rows) down or up by its row, once; the panes beside it don't move. Over a pane expanded with the sidebar hidden, the bar's row starts past the title buttons, so the pane's own header is a row lower.
- Tests' plain apps need the host (`themed` has it) to show a toast.

## Alternatives considered
- Toasts at the top right with the card, stacked: one column for every notice, but news would sit far from where the eye is, and a corner toast is easy to miss.
- Snack bars kept, turned upside down with a margin: Flutter's snack bar lays out from the bottom of the Scaffold; a top margin as tall as the window is fragile and still under a dialog.
