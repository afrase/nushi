# 0111. Surfaces, not outlines: floating panes on the window

Status: accepted, 2026-11-11.

## Context
The owner found the app's look dated and approved a mock of a fresh one (2026-11-11), wanting the app to look like it. ADR 0110 made the corners rounder; this page is the rest of the look, built the same day in five passes on one branch (the frame, the transcript and composer, the shared controls with Changes and the Pull request tab, then every view left, the phone and the Mac's window buttons) and changed where the owner saw a pass and said so: the details stay flat inside the pane, the composer's Stop is the old one, and what Nushi copied from Claude's desktop app keeps its structure. Every view is in it; this is its reference.

## Decision
- **Things are told apart by their fill, a step at a time, not by a 1 px border.** The steps are tokens of `theme/tokens.dart`, in every theme and both brightnesses:

  | Token | What is filled with it | Nushi dark / light |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `window` | the window behind everything; the sidebar sits straight on it | `#131417` / `#ECEEF2` |
  | `base` | a pane: the main area; a phone's screen | `#1B1C20` / `#FFFFFF` |
  | `raised` | a card on a pane or on the window (New chat, the status card, a widget of the details), a segmented control's track, a neutral pill, and what floats: a menu, a dialog, a popover | `#24262B` / `#F3F4F7` |
  | `lifted` | a raised bit: a button and a menu field wherever they sit, a key's cap, a track on a card or in a dialog, a tooltip, a toast | `#2F3138` / `#E6E8ED` |
  | `segment` | the picked segment of a segmented control, with a 1 px shadow under it | `#383B43` / `#FFFFFF` |
  | `codeBlock` | code, sunk: a code block, a tool call's output, a diff in the transcript or on a card | `#151619` / `#ECEEF2` |

  A theme that doesn't give one gets it from its `base`, `raised` and `text`, and so does a theme file that names those but not these. `tokens_test` holds each step at least 1.8 apart (OKLab × 100; Nushi's quietest, a light card on the window, is 1.9), code's surface that far from a pane and from a card, since it sits on both, and text to AA on every one of them.
- **A hairline is `line`** (the text's color at 7% dark, 9% light), and only: a divider inside a pane (under a header, between the transcript and a pane beside it, over the terminal, between a card's rows or a table's), and around the composer. **A border stays** only on a text field, which has to read as one on any surface (`fieldBorder`, `fieldBorderHover` under the pointer, `focus` with the caret, `crashed` in error), and as a focus ring (`focus`, 2 px). `borderSubtle` and `borderStrong`, the old outlines, are gone from the tokens.
- **State is a tint, never a box**: under the pointer `hover`, pressed `pressed`, the open row or view `accentSoft` with its label semibold, a toggle that's on `accentSoft` with its icon in `accentInk` (`IconTap(lit:)`). A state in words is a pill (`PhasePill`): the state's ink on its soft fill (`needs` on `needsSoft`, `running` on `accentSoft`, `done` on `doneSoft`, `crashed` on `crashedSoft`), a state that asks for nothing `textSecondary` on `raised`. A link shown as a pill is `PillLink` (`accentInk` on `accentSoft`).
- **A control is its fill, never an outline** (`controls.dart`). `Button`'s kinds: `primary`, the one main action, solid `accent` (a shade lighter under the pointer, `accentPressed` pressed); `secondary`, every other action, `lifted`; `quiet`, an action that stands back beside a filled one (an inline box's Cancel), no fill until the pointer is on it; `destructive`, `crashed` text on `lifted`, on `crashedSoft` under the pointer (its red is under AA on a tinted `lifted`); `danger`, `crashed` on `crashedSoft`. Every label is medium weight. `MenuField` is `lifted` with its ▾; `compact` and `pill` have no fill. Under the pointer a filled control takes `hover` over its fill, pressed `pressed`; disabled it's at 45%; focused from the keyboard it wears the ring. `tokens_test` holds `lifted` a step off a pane and a card, and text, muted text and `crashed` to AA on it.
- **What floats over the window is a card with a soft shadow**: a menu and its submenu, a dialog, the palette, New chat, the update card, the find bar, the reply box, the pins' and the outline's menus are `floatBox`: `raised`, a hairline where its edge meets what's under it (`floatLine`: `line`, twice as strong on a dark set, where a menu over the composer lies on a card of its own fill and the shadow doesn't show), and `floatShadows` (a wide soft shadow and a tight one, `floatShadow`: the theme's `shadow`, twice as deep on a dark set, where a soft one is lost). Material's own (menus, dialogs) get the same from the theme, at `floatElevation`. A tooltip is `lifted` with a small shadow and no hairline. A toast (a snack bar) is `lifted` with a hairline and no shadow: Flutter clips a snack bar to its own box, which cuts a shadow off hard at its sides. A dialog's scrim is Material's (black at 54%); Settings' is lighter, so a theme picked shows behind it.
- **The main area floats.** The window is `window`, with `NushiTheme.windowPad` (8) around the pane. The main area is one pane (`Pane`: `base`, corners `panel` 14, its content cut to them): the bars that come and go, the view's header, the view, and for a chat its side panes, its details and its terminal, hairlines between. Radii nest: a pane 14, a card 12, a row or control 8, the picked segment `inner(control, 3)`.
- **A chat's details are inside its pane, each widget a rounded card.** The header runs across the whole pane, and the details are a column under it at the right, 320 wide, behind a hairline (the owner, 2026-11-11, on the frame pass's cards beside the pane: "the old design did not move and I think that's how it should work"), so the header and its buttons stay where they are as the details show and hide. In the column each widget is a card of its own (`cardBox`: `raised`, corners `card`), 8 in from the column's sides and top and 8 from the next, its text 16 in from the column's sides as before; a folded widget is a short card, its title in its middle. No hairlines between them, and nothing in a card is a card or outlined: a fill set into one is the pane's (`base`), a closed pull request's note. The owner had them flat for a few hours ("I liked the more flat ones from Claude desktop that we copied"), then, with the two beside each other: "I think we should go to the rounded right widget on second thought." The mock's separate rail cards, beside the pane, stay dropped.
- **A toggle never moves when pressed.** The header's buttons are at the same place whatever they show or hide (`frame_test` holds the details button's): nothing a button opens may change the width of the bar it's in.
- **The sidebar has no pane and no border.** Its top strip is as high as the window's margin and a header (64 on the Mac, a row of 32 elsewhere): the traffic lights sit at its left and Arrange and the hide button at its right, all level with the header's middle; New chat is a `WideButton` (38 high, `raised`, its key a `KeyCap`); a chat's row is 34 high with 2 between, its title `textSecondary`, the open one `text` semibold on `accentSoft`; a group's name is a small muted label (11.5, semibold, `textMuted`) with its collapse chevron after it, shown under the pointer, with the focus, or while collapsed; Jira, GitHub and Settings are quiet rows 32 high, with no line over them (the list fades out at its end); a chat that needs you says so in a small pill at its row's end, where the row has room. Its foot is the status card (`StatusBar(card:)`): the agents' counts and the memory. With the sidebar hidden the pane reaches the window's left margin, its header starting past the lights, and the same counts and memory are a line under the pane on the window's background.
- **The Mac's traffic lights are level with the headers**, 36 from the window's top (its margin and half a header) and 20 in from its left, where the sidebar's rows' words start: the same place with the sidebar shown, in its top strip, and hidden, in the pane's header, whose row starts past them (95 from the window's edge). AppKit has no setting for it and puts them back at its own place (9 in, the middle of a title bar 32 high) whenever it lays its title bar out, so `WindowButtons` (`macos/Runner/TitleBar.swift`) makes the bar's container 72 high and centres them in it, on each resize in the same pass and on each of the window's updates. In full screen they are AppKit's own, in the bar that drops down with the menu bar.
- **A header is `PaneHeader.height` (56) high, with a hairline under it and no fill**: the chat's, a view's, a side pane's, so their lines meet. The chat's row starts at the transcript's gutter (24) and ends 12 from the pane's edge: title 14 semibold, the Jira issue a `PillLink`, the agent's mark and name, the state a `PhasePill` with its dot, then `Segmented(roomy:)` for Chat, Changes and Pull request (a `raised` track, the picked segment `segment`, a count as a small accent pill), and icon buttons 32 square (`IconTap.header`), lit while what they show is open. The sidebar's button is the same control, past the lights at a header's left while the sidebar is hidden and beside Arrange (the same again) in the sidebar's strip while it's shown, so a header's two ends match; its icon is where it was in its smaller box, 5 in, the box reaching 3 past that.
- **No colored stripe down the side of a card, a row or a group** (the owner's rule). A chat's color was a bar at its row's left; it is a small lozenge at the row's end (`ColorChip`, 12 × 6, round ends so it reads apart from the round state dots), the same before the title in the header while the sidebar is hidden, and on the phone's list.
- **The transcript**. Its column is 800 wide at most (`chatWidth`), the composer's too. Prose is 14 on 22; inline code is the warm ink on its tint, its corners square (a text run's background has none). What the agent did is a card's fill, never a tint or an outline:
  - **A tool call's row** (`Collapsible`): one line 36 high on `raised`, corners `control`; its icon `toolAccent` (`crashed`, with the word "failed", once it failed), its words `textSecondary`, a command and a path in code type, `+n −m` and a small pill (`PillButton(small:)`: Preview, Open) at its end, then a spinner while it runs. Open, it's a card (`card`), its header where it was, and its output, input and diffs in one block of `codeBlock` set 4 in (`inner(card, 4)`). **A run** opens to a card of its calls' rows, 34 high with no fill of their own; an open one is the `codeBlock` block, header and all. A diff there has `codeBlock` for its background (`diffTheme(surface:)`), no frame.
  - **A subagent's row** is `subagentTint` (the one tint: it's another agent's work), its name `subagentAccent` semibold, its state at the end; `accentSoft` while its pane is open, as any open row, `crashedSoft` once it failed.
  - **The plan** is a card (`cardBox`: `raised`, `card`): "Plan · 2 of 4 done" and that count as a pill-shaped bar (`done` on `lifted`); a done step muted with a check, the running one a dot of `running` in an `accentSoft` halo, medium, one to come a ring.
  - **A message of yours** is a bubble of `userBand` (`panel`, 12 × 18; a band with no lines on the phone). **Thinking** is a dim italic line with a chevron; open, its text is set in under the label.
  - **Code blocks** are `codeBlock` with no outline; a table has hairlines between its rows only; an image has no outline (`raised` shows through a see-through one).
  - **Cards that wait on you** (a permission, a question, the trust card) are `cardBox` too. That one waits is its amber mark and a "Needs you" pill (`NeedsYou`, the header's and the sidebar's words), and a ring that glows once as it comes; answered, the pill goes. The one main answer is a solid button, the rest secondary ones (`lifted`, no outline). A findings card is a card with hairlines between its rows, its chips `lifted` pills.
  - **What floats over the text** (Jump to latest, a queued message's pill) is a pill on `lifted` or `raised`, the chip with a soft shadow, no outline.
- **No bar down a quote's side either.** Quoted text (a Markdown quote, the selection a reply quotes) is set on `quote`, a tint of the text's color with a control's corners, which shows on the pane, in a bubble and on a card alike; opened Thinking is an indent. The Markdown quote's bar was the one conventional stripe, and the fill reads as well.
- **The composer** is a card: `raised`, a `line` hairline, corners `panel`, a soft shadow. The text is 14, and under it, inside the box, is one row 32 high: a round + (`RoundButton`, `lifted`), the agent's pickers as quiet pills with a ▾ (`MenuField(pill:)`: mode, model and effort, then ⋯ and Ultracode), and at its end a round Send on `accent` once something is written, or while a turn runs Queue (⏎) and Stop and send (⌘⏎) as pills (`PillButton`), then Stop. Stop is the app's own from before the redesign, unchanged by it: a filled square in a circle in `textSecondary`, in a round area 28 across that tints under the pointer (the owner, 2026-11-11, of the mock's round red one: "the old one was fine"). The words are the glossary's: the mock's "Steer" is Stop and send. Under the box is only a new chat's folder and New worktree chip. A box under 400 wide (a side chat's), and a phone's at any width (on its side it has no height for two rows), is one row instead, the text between the + and the buttons, its pickers under it; under 620 Queue and Stop and send are round icon buttons.
- **Changes**. Its bar is 44 high with no line under it: the scopes are the filled control (`Segmented`, as the header's), then the files' count and lines, Send to agent (a primary button) and the list's, the diff's and Reload's icon buttons, 12 from the pane's edges. The file list is on the pane itself, with no fill and no divider (its edge shows under the pointer, to drag): a row is 26 high, the open file `accentSoft` with its name semibold. The diff is a block of `codeBlock` beside it, 8 in from the pane's right and bottom, corners `control`; with no file to show there's no block. A diff on a surface keeps its tints: each line's and ribbon's fill is as far off the surface as the theme's is off its own diff background (`diffTheme(surface:)`), or a light set's grey for a deleted line would vanish on `codeBlock`. Hairlines only over the commit box and under the commits and the comments above the list. The reply cards waiting are a banner of `accentSoft` set 8 in, corners `control`.
- **A review comment is a card with a mark, not a bar**: `raised`, corners `control`, set 8 in between the diff's lines; a comment's mark in `accentInk` before its lines. Its state is a pill: an outdated one's mark is a clock's in `needs` with "Outdated" on `needsSoft`, a sent one says "Sent" on `lifted` (and is dimmed, as before). The box to write one in is the same card, its Cancel a quiet button beside Comment.
- **The Pull request tab** keeps its page and its words; its parts are cards. The four status tiles are `raised` cards with no line along their top (it tapered in the round corners): a tile's state is its mark in a round chip of the state's soft fill (`crashedSoft`, `needsSoft`, `doneSoft`; `lifted` for one that asks for nothing) and its value in the state's ink. To do is one card with hairlines between its rows, its count and a plain tag (Required) `lifted` pills, a state's tag its tint; a row's Log, Reply and Resolve are secondary buttons beside the solid Send. A comment in Activity is a card with a hairline under its head; a resolved thread, a pointer chip, a check's row and the Merge control are cards or fills with no outline, a failed check saying so with its own mark. A branch's name is a `raised` tag. Its sections stay `PlainTabs`: they're places on one page, under a body of cards.
- **Settings, Jira, GitHub and Chat history** keep their pages and their words; their boxes are cards. A settings group, a status's or an inbox's card, the history table and a form to add an account are `cardBox` (`raised`, corners `card`), hairlines between their rows, no outline. Settings is a pane (`base`) that floats over the window; its nav is behind a hairline, and its open section is the open row (`accentSoft`, semibold), as the open file is in a pull request's Files and in the files pane (there the keys' place, when it's another row, is `selected`). A theme to pick is a `lifted` card with its picture, ringed in `accentInk` while it's the one picked; the picture draws this look (a bubble, a tool row on `raised`, a sunk code block). A Jira status is its state's ink on the state's soft fill (`statusSoft`: `doneSoft`, `accentSoft`, `lifted` for one to do), filled only under the pointer in a list. A pull request's Files section is Changes' layout: the list on the pane, the diff a block of `codeBlock` beside it. The history table's header, pinned, has the pane's fill behind its round corners, so no row shows past them.
- **A switch is its fill** (the theme's `switchTheme`): off, `lifted` with a `textMuted` thumb; on, `accent`. **An empty checkbox keeps an edge**, as a field does, but a field's (`fieldBorderHover`, 1.5), not Material's two points of the text's color.
- **A notice is a soft fill, never an outlined box**: a warning `needsSoft` with its mark in `needs` (what a clean-up would lose, a list that's old), a choice to make `accentSoft`, a pull request's merged note `subagentTint` (GitHub's purple). A meter's track and a skeleton's bars are `lifted`; a grey mark that stands back (the empty window's logo, a rail's dashes, the part of a bar that counts for nothing) is `faint`, the pane's fill a third of the way to the text.
- **The phone** takes the surfaces' colors and the chip, and stays one column with no floating panes: a screen is `base` with a hairline under its bar, a button `lifted` (the main one the accent's), a sheet `raised`, a theme to pick a `raised` card with the same ring, every target 44 points as before. A chat's state in its list stays a word in the state's ink. Its box is one row, so no hint may wrap it: while a turn runs it says "Queue it, or stop and send" (the longer words wrapped at 390 wide once the text was 14).

## Consequences
- `raised` went from "a panel or a bar" to "a card": what filled a bar with it (a pane's header, the sidebar, the status bar) has no fill now. Nushi's `base` and `raised` are darker in the dark set, and `raised` a little darker in the light one; Solarized's light cards and Catppuccin's dark ones took new tones, Nord's light window is nord4.
- A row's text sits on the window, so each light set's muted text, and two of its marks, were taken darker where they fell under AA there (Nushi's `textMuted` is the mock's).
- Headers are 12 higher (44 until now, ADR 0072), and the chat's row takes about 70 more before its title, so its parts fold in a window about that much wider; the state's word now folds before the tabs do, which stay a click away longer. Changes' bar takes its short labels in a narrow pane without the worktrees' menu too.
- The window's margins come out of the transcript: beside the details it keeps 484, not 500, so they hide at the window widths they did (ADR 0092).
- The composer is 86 high with one line (48 until now), and a tool row 36 (28): a screen shows fewer rows, as the mock does. The transcript's column is 60 narrower.
- A waiting card is no longer amber all over, and a failed call's row no longer red: each says it with a mark and a word in the state's color. A running call has a spinner again beside its shine.
- `codeBlock` is darker than the pane on every dark set, and about the window's tone on the light ones (the mock's light value was under a step from a card, where a diff's block would vanish); Nord's dark bubble's muted ink is lighter, to read on a quote there. GitHub's and Jira's text draw quotes, code blocks and tables the same way, as they share the Markdown.
- Eight tokens are gone, with nothing left to draw them: `borderSubtle`, `borderStrong`, `toolTint`, `toolBorder`, `subagentBorder`, `findingsTint`, `findingsBorder` and `userBandBorder`. A theme file that still names them reads as it did: they're passed over without a note (`retiredTokens`). One came, `faint`, worked out from `base` and `text`, which no file names.
- The sidebar's New chat and chats start 24 lower on the Mac (at the header's line, 64, not 40), and a header with the sidebar hidden starts 11 further in.
- Amends 0071 (the header's switch is the filled control again, as the mock has it), 0072 (the header's and the composer's heights and fold numbers), 0076 (a bubble's padding; a tool row's fill and height), 0080 (the top is the sidebar's strip and the pane's header, not one band; the traffic lights are moved after all, and put back on every resize) and 0092 (the details hide where the transcript would keep less than 484).

## Left
- **The sidebar's projects** as tiles with a rail, as the mock's Projects page has them: a separate decision (`docs/roadmap.md`).
- **Not seen on a real window**: the traffic lights in full screen and on the way out of it (nothing is placed there, and they're put back as it ends), and a resize by hand (`make test-window` resizes a window that's never shown).

## Alternatives considered
- Renaming `base` and `raised` to `pane` and `card`: every theme file names them, and some 110 lines of the app.
- `control` at the mock's 10: at 26 to 34 high a row or a button reads as bubbly (ADR 0110), and 8 nests well enough in a pane's 14.
- The lights at the mock's 26 from the top, in a sidebar row 28 high: the header is 56 high under the window's margin, so they'd be level with nothing in it once the sidebar hides, which is where they looked dropped.
- A strip across the top for the lights while the sidebar is hidden: the owner took the title bar away for wasting that space (ADR 0080).
- Dropping the counts and the memory with the sidebar: the status bar was the only place for them.
- The details as cards beside the pane, as the mock has them (the frame pass): the pane's edge, and the header's buttons with it, moved each time they were shown or hidden.
- The details' widgets flat in their column, hairlines between, as Claude's rail (ADR 0092, and this page for a few hours): the owner chose the rounded cards once both were drawn.
- The composer's corners at the mock's 20, a tool row's at its 10: neither is one of ADR 0110's tokens, and `panel` and `control` nest with the pane.
- A waiting card filled with `needsSoft`, as before: the look says a state with a pill or a mark, and a tinted card makes every button on it a different grey.
- A round Stop filled solid red (the transcript pass, with `stop` and `onStop` tokens), as the mock has it: the owner didn't want it bright red, and the tokens went with it.
- Queue and Stop and send only as icons, as before: on a phone they were the only way to do either, and nothing said what they were.
